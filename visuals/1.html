The Infinite Potential Well

Interactive Graph: Use the sliders in the left panel of the calculator below to adjust the parameters.
  • \(n\) is the principal quantum number (determines the energy state).
  • \(a\) is the width of the quantum well in nanometers.
  • The Grey Areas represent the infinitely high potential walls outside the well.
  • The Blue Line represents the wave function \(\psi(x)\), which perfectly collapses to zero at the boundaries.
  • The Red Shaded Area represents the probability density \(|\psi(x)|^2\). Notice the "nodes" where probability is zero for \(n > 1\).

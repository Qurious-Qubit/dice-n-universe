Visuals

Interactive computational models developed to illustrate core quantum and solid‑state concepts. Each tool is built using foundational logic to visually represent the underlying mathematics.

  • 1D Infinite Quantum Well – bound states and wavefunctions
  • Finite Quantum Well – bound states with tunnelling tails
  • Kronig‑Penney Model – energy bands in periodic potential
  • Effective Mass – curvature of \(E\)‑\(k\) relation
  • Conduction & Valence Bands – band gap visualisation

Further models detailing Fermi levels and TCAD‑style MOSFET band diagrams are currently in development.

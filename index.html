Hardware design & solid‑state physics – building a living notebook at the intersection of determinism and quantum probability.

This site is my technical log: a collection of notes, simulations, and visual explanations on semiconductor physics, quantum mechanics, and device engineering. It is intended for fellow engineers, researchers, and admissions committees who value clarity and depth.

Why “dice‑n‑universe”?

The name echoes the famous Einstein‑Bohr debate. In solid‑state electronics, we deal with the deterministic behaviour of engineered devices (the universe Einstein trusted) and the underlying quantum probabilistic nature that makes them work (the dice Bohr embraced). This tension is where I find my focus.

What you’ll find here

  • Notes – structured notes on band theory, carrier statistics, MOSFET physics, etc.
  • Visualizations – interactive simulations: quantum well, Kronig‑Penney model, wave packets, and more.
  • My background – hardware design, testing, and research interests (see Vitae & Contact for CV and LinkedIn).

This is a work in progress. New notes and simulations are added regularly. If you’d like to discuss a topic or collaboration, feel free to reach out.
